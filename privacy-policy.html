Marble Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-11

Data Collection

Marble Rush does not require account sign-in and does not collect personal information directly from players.

Local Device Storage

The game stores gameplay preferences and progress on your device only (for example best score, settings, and achievements) using local storage.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

The Android build can be configured to send anonymous usage events and crash diagnostics to improve game stability and performance. These integrations are optional and disabled unless configured by the publisher.

Third-Party Services

If enabled by the publisher, third-party services may process technical data under their own privacy terms:

  • Firebase Analytics (anonymous gameplay events)
  • Sentry (crash diagnostics)
  • RevenueCat / Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

Children's Privacy

Marble Rush is not intended to collect personal data from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the publisher via the Google Play listing support email.
